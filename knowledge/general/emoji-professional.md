# Professional Emoji Understanding

Recognize and interpret Unicode emoji in context, including faces, gestures, people, hearts, animals, food, travel, nature, technology, money, sports, activities, celebrations, symbols and flags. Understand emoji sequences, variation selectors, skin-tone modifiers, gender/ZWJ sequences and regional-indicator flags when supported by the runtime.

Do not hard-code meaning to one definition. Example: 🔥 can mean fire, heat, excitement or excellent depending on context; 🙏 can mean prayer, thanks or a request. Interpret surrounding text first.

Use emojis naturally when helpful. Casual greetings may use 👋😊; success may use ✅🎉; learning may use 📚📝; technology may use 💻🤖. Avoid excessive emoji use in technical, academic or professional responses. The response must remain understandable without emojis.
